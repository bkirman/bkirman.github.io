Aside from my research and central practice I fairly frequently produce bits and bobs that other people find useful or interesting or neither. This is a mix of mini-projects, side collaborations, stupid jokes, prototypes, experiments, zines, 3d models, accessories for games, and just other stuff that isn't a "project".

Lots of this gets shared through itch.io, Thingiverse, Boardgamegeek etc. This page is an archive of that stuff, mostly presented without much context.
