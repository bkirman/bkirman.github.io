Last updated 1/7/26

## Research
* Been a busy few months, organising the [Museum of Manky Tea Towels](http://manky.website) with Oliver for the [Electromagnetic Field](https://www.emfcamp.org) festival. This is a really nice alternative way to have an "output" for the background research we've been doing in tea towels as critical media.
* I'm also running a workshop at Electromagnetic Field for people interested in making games with 1-dimensional LED strips. It should be fun, and is a good opportunity for me to do more experimental game design with these things. I love them and it just seems there is more to explore.
* Again with Oliver, and also Marcia and David, I recently published a paper about [Research through Game Design](https://doi.org/10.21606/drs.2026.565) at the [Design Research Society](https://designresearchsociety.org) conference in Edinburgh. It was nice to get this out as an expression of doing more "silly" and unfinished things, and me and Oliver have been talking about writing something on the topic for years. It has also had some really nice feedback from students which is great. DRS is still a bit of an odd venue for game design research but there were a handful of game papers there.
