Last updated 8/7/25

## Research
* Last year was a huge one for [PlayOn!](/projects/play-on). The project concluded in October 2024 but it was a really busy time working with partners across Europe and making a load of cool stuff I can't wait to share. The job now is to convert all those notebooks, photographs and interviews into a useful format for sharing. This has proved a massive task and is going to take a while to unpick. There were 33 shows, 9 countries, and thousands of audience members.
* In the meantime I attended [British DiGRA](https://bdigra.co.uk/what-is-british-games-research) in Birmingham in May 2025, which was really a blast. Oliver and I talked about Research through Game Design, Meal Deal, Tea towels and shared [a zine we made](https://oliverbates.itch.io/rtgd).
* I also got to visit [A MAZE in Berlin](https://2025.amaze-berlin.de/), which was similarly inspirational, fun and weird in a good way. I didn't present so could just absorb it all, between meetings with various Berlin colleagues.
