Other Bits

Aside from my research and central practice I am pretty much always working on tiny bits of creative stuff - a mix of mini-projects, side collaborations, stupid jokes, prototypes, experiments, zines, 3d models, accessories for games, and just other odds and ends. Even though these aren't "projects" that I think are particularly significant, I wanted a way to keep track of it all for myself.

Lots of this gets shared through itch.io, Thingiverse, Boardgamegeek etc. This page is an archive of that stuff all in one place. They are half-ideas or things that make no sense out of context, and that's fine for me. The bigger/better ideas are on the projects tab.

Radio Explorer for Home Assistant (2025)

Mean Dice (2025)
A set of 3d-printable dice for RPGs and other games. The dice are engineered to have statistically consistent outcomes. VERY consistent.
