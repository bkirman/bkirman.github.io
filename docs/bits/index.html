Other Bits

Aside from my research and central practice I fairly frequently produce bits and bobs that other people find useful or interesting or neither. This is a mix of mini-projects, side collaborations, stupid jokes, prototypes, experiments, zines, 3d models, accessories for games, and just other stuff that isn't a "project".

Lots of this gets shared through itch.io, Thingiverse, Boardgamegeek etc. This page is an archive of that stuff, mostly presented without much context.

Mean Dice (2025)
A set of 3d-printable dice for RPGs and other games. The dice are engineered to have statistically consistent outcomes. VERY consistent.
Imperium Tokens (2025)
3d-printable tokens for Imperium Classics/Legends/Horizons
Making a Meal out of a Mountain (2025)
A zine about doing Research through Game Design, presented at British DiGRA (with Oliver Bates)
Screw That (2025)
A zine game about collapse, and living with resource scarcity in the home (with Oliver Bates)
